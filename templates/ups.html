Online UPS

Double-conversion protection for critical loads.

For servers, medical equipment, control rooms and precision machinery, online double-conversion UPS delivers perfectly regulated power with zero transfer time. We specify, size and install systems with the right runtime, redundancy and battery configuration — then keep them healthy through scheduled preventive maintenance.

Offline UPS

Dependable backup for everyday equipment.

Offices, shops, labs and homes need clean switchover without the cost of online topology. Offline and line-interactive UPS protect desktops, POS systems, networking and household essentials from outages and voltage swings — a cost-effective shield we stock across popular capacities.

Industrial UPS

Hardened power for demanding environments.

Factories, process lines and infrastructure demand UPS built for harsh electrical environments. We supply and maintain industrial-grade systems with robust inverters, extended battery banks and service access designed around production schedules — engineered to trip nothing but the failure itself.

Batteries

The right battery, properly maintained.

A UPS is only as reliable as its batteries. We supply quality battery banks matched to each system, test load and autonomy annually, replace ageing cells on schedule and dispose of spent batteries responsibly — so backup capacity is real when the power actually fails.

AMC & Servicing
