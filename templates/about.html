Who We Are

An energy partner Tamil Nadu has trusted for nearly three decades.

GV POWERS is an established energy and power solutions company operating from Chennai and Salem, Tamil Nadu. Since our founding in 1997, we have focused on one thing — keeping our customers powered through dependable engineering, honest advice and service they can count on.

Over the years our work has grown from power backup into complete energy solutions: rooftop and ground-mounted solar with turnkey EPC execution, online and industrial UPS systems, and diesel and silent generators. More than 1,250 UPS units and 1,800 generators sold, alongside a growing portfolio of solar projects, reflect the trust our customers place in us.

We deliberately stay close to our customers. Our engineers survey every site, design for real operating conditions, and remain available long after commissioning through service visits and annual maintenance contracts. That relationship-first approach is why so much of our work comes from customers who have been with us for years.
